♠PacksA TABLE FOR TWO

A little strategy. A shared table.

  1. Each player gets a private hand of twelve cards and three face-down stacks of four. Four more cards make the kitty.
  2. The non-dealer bids first. Each player gets one bid or pass; the dealer must bid after an opening pass. Equal numbers rank low above high.
  3. The bid winner exchanges the kitty and calls trump. A dealer calls before seeing the kitty; a non-dealer calls after discarding four.
  4. A pack has four plays: leader’s hand, follower’s hand, leader’s table, follower’s table. Follow the original suit whenever possible, separately within your hand or exposed table cards.
  5. Trump beats other suits. High ranks A–K–Q…2; low ranks A–2–3…K. The strongest single qualifying card wins the pack and the next lead.
  6. The defender needs 8 minus the bid, with a minimum of one pack. Otherwise play all twelve packs. BOLO and REVERSE BOLO score two games; other wins score one. First to seven or more wins.
